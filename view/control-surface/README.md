# view/control-surface

The views' jacks, in the views' own words: what their models read, each an in, labelled as a jack on a module's panel is, by its direction; what's done goes out on the app's bus as each plan's message. Whatever answers an in fits it by its shape. Before the view models in the import order, so a model, its drawer and what answers it share one jack.

| | |
|---|---|
| `plan.ts` | a plan's jacks: `PlanIn`, what the views read of a plan as it plays, by address, its values, starts, roles, slots, copies and meters (the view models' `ModelPort` until SHAPE5b3); `Role`, how a value is set, live here, automated by what its track plays, fixed as built; `Slot`, what a device does in its slot and what sets it there; `Command`, what's done in a view, by address, a param's, which the tool records through the sequencer and sets, a setting's, a rebuild, not recorded, sent as the app's message for the plan (the view models' `command.ts` until SHAPE5b3) |
| `desk.ts` | `deskIn()`: a desk's in as it plays (`libs/build/desk/`'s `Desk`), as `PresetIn` is a preset's: a value as it plays, a strip's mute and solo its live mix's; its start; how a param is set and what a slot does there, worked out (SHAPE5a0c4b): on a live strip an insert's own macros, params and settings live, deeper ones fixed; on a track by what its setup says sets it, fixed where nothing does; a slot's job its plan's words (`does`), else on its strip, what sets it on a track its sources; a level and a reading once built, its channel there, by the meters it asks of what's built; a pool's copies; what's done sent as the desk's message (`deskEdited()`, SHAPE5b2; a param handed to where its owner said a move goes before) (SHAPE1b3; the studio's since LAY12d1; the view models' `desk-port.ts`, `deskPort()`, until SHAPE5b3). Tested in node on the desk library's desk and stand-in (`test/desk.test.js`); a param set by its notes recorded, its automation in the arrangement's lanes (`Role.recorded`, SHAPE4d3) |
| `preset.ts` | `PresetIn`: a plan's in for a plan whose values a preset gives, a patch's on its voice card: each value the preset's, its start as it began, how each is set as its owner says, else by the macro that moves it, else live; what's done sent as the preset's message by whoever makes its models (`PresetOwner`, LAY15a; its owner's `changed()` until SHAPE5b2; the view models' `preset-port.ts`, `PresetPort`, until SHAPE5b3) |
| `ClipSlots` | clips in slots on tracks, as what holds them knows them: a track's slots in the order put (`slotsOn(track)`), whether a slot's clip plays (`playing(slot)`); a clip player (`libs/clip-player/`) answers it for its slots, a control surface for the clips it holds on no player (`clips.ts`) |
| `ClipTrack`, `HeldClips`, `ClipsIn` | a clip track as a view shows it: its key, its name, the roles whose instruments play its clips, whether its clips are launched and deleted from the view, and where they're held, each what holds them and their track there; the control surface the clips were played on, its clip tracks in order, a clip's name and how many notes it holds (`clips.ts`; `ClipSurface` until SHAPE5b3) |

Its readers: the view models (`libs/view/models/`), the session view's, the device view's and the clip tracks'. Tested in node, in its `test/` folder: a desk's in on the desk library's desk and stand-in (`test/desk.test.js`).
