# libs/view/mixer/dom

A desk drawn in the DOM, on a device's drawers (`libs/view/device/dom/`).

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| `section-view.ts` | `SectionView`: a section of a view, its label and its strips in a row; one that folds, its label an arrow and a dot, folded to its first strip by a click on its label, with shift every section beside it (LAY12h2), which are folded a model's (`Toggles`), by each section's label, shared by every section beside it, drawn from it (SHAPE5a0a3a), its light on while a hidden strip sounds, by what it sounds as (`data-sounds`) (LAY12h1, the session view's `sections.ts`) |
| `mixer-view.ts`, `mixer-html.ts` | `MixerView`: a mixer drawn in its sections as the sound flows, the tracks, the buses, the FX busses and the master: the groups of tracks the view hands it, as the session's tracks model shows them (`MixerGroup` and `MixerTrack`), the desk's own channels as one where it's handed none, a track's cell drawn from what it plays now, lit while it sounds, dimmed while it waits, each in a box kept across drawings, its scroll and its width in tracks, folded to its first track until it's unfolded, which a model's (`Toggles`, SHAPE5a0a3a); the desk's own channels from the mixer model (`deskHtml()`), a bus with those that go into it in a box; a track's strip, empty while it plays nothing (`trackHtml()`); a strip's devices opened by its address (`data-open`), its sends labelled by their returns' short labels; the level bars on every frame; the lights, by what the strips heard feed (`fedBy()`) (LAY12h1, the session view's `strips.ts` and `draw.ts`); its clicks its own, a strip's name or slot told by its address (`onOpen()`), mute, solo with shift, a group's fold, its knobs' drag (LAY12h2, the session view's `clicks.ts`) (its HTML in `mixer-html.ts`) |
| `insert-devices.ts` | `InsertDevices`: a strip's inserts, the master's too, a chain of the device view's, opened by the strip's address: each insert its owner says something of a composite drawn flat, in the order its sound goes through them, its status its label, its job and what sets it, a reset for what's live on it (SHAPE5a0a, the session view's `devices/inserts.ts`) |
| `strip-view.ts` | `stripHtml()`: a strip drawn from its model, its name, where it goes and what plays on it as the view says (`StripLook`), its slots, its sends, pan, fader, level bar, value, mute and solo; quiet where it's silent; `slotsHtml()`, its inserts' slots, each its device and what sets it; `muteSoloHtml()`, its mute and solo by its key |

Tested in node on HTML as text and on values, in its `test/` folder: `dom.test.js`, `mixer-view.test.js`.
