# libs/view/models

View models built from a plan, with no DOM: handed a plan and its values through a port, they hand back what a view shows of it and what it can do, as MVVM's view models do (`mockup/architecture/layout.md`, "How a plan is shown"). Reads only the plan's format and the params' specs, the clock for the arrangement and the clip tracks, and the clip slots' port (`libs/view/control-surface/`); tested in node. Since LAY12b.

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| `model.ts` | `Model`: the plan and its values by address, read through the tool's port (`ModelPort`): each value as it plays, the plan's where none was told; its start, which a reset brings back; how it's set (`Role`: live here, automated by what its track plays, fixed as built); a node's level as the implementation hears it, at one of its points where it has several, and a reading, a limiter's reduction (LAY12f); a pool's copies added so far; what's done, sent as a command |
| `view-builder.ts` | `ViewBuilder`: view models for whatever part of a plan is asked: a param or a setting as a control (`control()`), a node as a device (`device()`), a channel as a strip (`strip()`), a desk as a mixer (`mixer()`); a composite's children grouped by their roles, in the order they first come (`groups()`); what a composite's own sources move, a row a target, as a mod matrix (`matrix()`, `MatrixRow`); a level and a reading by address, as the implementation hears them (`peakOf()`, `readingOf()`); the plan they're built from (`plan`) |
| `knob.ts` | `Knob`: a param or a setting with a range as a control: its name, unit, range and taper its declared spec; its value, where it lies on its control by its taper, and its text, a gain in dB, a pan by its side, any other to its step; turned, set, toggled or reset, a command by its address where it's live, a gain to a hundredth, any other to its step; its value typed in, read as its text is written, a gain in dB, a pan by its side (`setText()`, LAY15a); its rings, the range each source moves it through, a macro its range mapped, a modulator from its value by its reach (`rings`, LAY15a) |
| `choice.ts` | `Choice`: a param or a setting with choices: its options and its value; chosen or reset, a command where it's live |
| `device.ts` | `Device`: a node as a device, its address, its name and kind, its controls in order: a composite's macros in its template's order, a block's params, then the settings within it; what every control has (`Control`), a knob's and a choice's |
| `strip.ts` | `Strip`: a channel as a strip, by the channel form's roles: its inserts as devices, its sends' knobs by return, its fader, pan, mute and solo, its level, quiet where its cut is at 0; its name as shown, its label, else its own, a pool's copy its pool's and its number (`shownName()`); its name in little room, its short label, else as shown (`shortName()`, a send's knob, LAY12h1); where it goes, the channel its way out feeds, by that one's name as shown (LAY12c) |
| `mixer.ts` | `Mixer`: a desk as a mixer, its strips by role in the plan's order: its buses, each with the buses that go out into it (`BusGroup`), its returns, its master; a solo click, as Ableton's, alone or with shift added or let go, a pool's copies among them (LAY12c); what strips feed, for a view's lights, each through its way out and the sends said to be up, a pool's copies wired by its rule among them, the master left out (`reached()`, LAY12f) |
| `moves.ts` | `PlanMoves`: what moves each param, worked out from a plan alone (`Move`): a macro, each target its maps move, by its factor and offset, held within its map's ends; a child wired into a param, a modulator, its reach its own amount or depth; a composite's own moves, for its matrix (LAY15a) |
| `envelope.ts` | `envelopeDrawing()`: an envelope's curve from its knobs, as the instrument plays it (the ARP's, `libs/plan/blocks/`), a level a step, its delay's end and the note's marked; drawn as long as it takes where it's asked no length, held until it has settled onto its sustain, then its release (LAY15a) |
| `preset-port.ts` | `PresetPort`: a port for a plan whose values a preset gives, a patch's on its voice card: each value the preset's, its start as it began, how each is set as its owner says, else by the macro that moves it, else live; what's done handed to its owner (`PresetOwner`, LAY15a) |
| `cards.ts` | `InstrumentCards`: an instrument's patches as the device view edits them, a card for each, its own or a drum rack's pads by their keys, each a `PresetPort` on its voice card planned at its preset, values from outside over its own (`CardValues`, the tweak panel's knobs), the note's sources set by the note; the patch edited in the bank, not an instrument playing it (SHAPE1a; the tool's since LAY15a). Tested in node on the tests' file of instruments (`test/cards.test.js`) |
| `desk-port.ts` | `deskPort()`: a port for a desk as it plays (`libs/build/desk/`'s `Desk`), as `PresetPort` is for a preset: a value as it plays, a strip's mute and solo its live mix's; its start; how a param is set and what a slot does there, worked out (SHAPE5a0c4b): on a live strip an insert's own macros, params and settings live, deeper ones fixed; on a track by what its setup says sets it, fixed where nothing does; a slot's job its plan's words (`does`), else on its strip, what sets it on a track its sources; a level and a reading once built, its channel there, by the meters it asks of what's built; a pool's copies; what's done, a param handed to where its owner says a move goes, a choice by its index, a setting a rebuild (SHAPE1b3; the studio's since LAY12d1). Tested in node on the desk library's desk and stand-in (`test/desk-port.test.js`); a param set by its notes recorded, its automation in the arrangement's lanes (`Role.recorded`, SHAPE4d3) |
| `command.ts` | `Command`: what's done in a view, by address: a param's, which the tool records through the sequencer and sets; a setting's, a rebuild, not recorded |
| `custom.ts` | `CustomViews`: a view of its own, registered by a node's kind, where a view is more than its params: a meter, the limiter's reduction, an envelope's curve; the composite view's (`libs/view/dom/`) |
| `clip-tracks.ts` | `ClipTracks`: the clip tracks, as a session view shows them, a clip track for each of a control surface's (`libs/view/control-surface/`): each clip track's clips read from what holds them, a clip player's slots or the surface's own for the clips on none, in the order held, each once, its name, its note count and whether it plays, its holder's to say (`ShownClip`); a clip's ▶ launching it, its × asking first, a second click after a moment and within 3 s deleting it, anything else or 3 s unanswered stopping it asking, through the functions it's handed (SHAPE4c) |
| `session.ts` | `Session`: what the session view shows of what's played, read in its own words, the MIDI sequencer's stream fitting them by their shape: plays kept per group of tracks, a group as many tracks as its plays ever used, your playing on its kind's pool, a replay on its group (`groupOf()`, handed in); a look at a time (`at()`), each track what holds it and whether it sounded since, or the replay waiting for its first note, each group's clips that sounded; a loop holding its track while it plays, a held note until its note off, by its sender (SHAPE5a0a, the session view's `session-view/session.ts`) |
| `session-tracks.ts` | `SessionTracks`, `SessionGroup`, `SessionTrack`, `TrackNow`: the session's tracks as its mixer shows them, from a look at the session in the control surface's groups (`Tracks`): the tracks with notes, in the groups' order, yours marked, a group shown once its first play adds a track; each track named from its group's name and numbered from 1, at its channel's address on the desk (handed in), what it plays now, its clip and a visitor's motif after it, a dash with none, lit while it sounds, waiting while a replay waits for its first note; what sounded since the last look, a replay group's clips and each lit track's channel, for the folded sections' lights (SHAPE5a0d4a, the session view's `draw.ts` before) |
| `toggles.ts` | `Toggles`: keys turned from where each starts, what a view folds or opens by key, a section folded, a group unfolded, a track's lanes opened; one turned alone, or every one handed in as the one clicked goes (SHAPE5a0a3a; the arrangement's `toggled()` before) |
| `views.ts` | `Views`: the session view and the arrangement, which one is open, or none, a tab each opening and closing it, another's closing as this one opens leaving it shown; whoever listens told as it changes (SHAPE5a0a3a, out of the drawer) |
| `meters.ts` | `meterFall()`: a level bar's fall, as a DIN peak meter's, up at once to its peak, back down 20 dB in 1.5 s, silence below -80 dB; `Meters`, each bar's level kept by its key, falling (SHAPE5a0a3c, `libs/view/dom/meter-view.ts`'s `Levels` before) |
| `limiter-history.ts` | `LimiterHistory`: the limiter's history, oldest first, a column each interval, the loudest in, out and taken away of its frames (`Peaks`), the oldest let go, silent at first; a long frame its peaks in one column, then silence (SHAPE5a0a3c, out of `LimiterView`) |
| `chosen.ts` | `Chosen`: what's chosen in the composites a view shows, as a synth's panel keeps the page you were on: each rack's pad, by the rack's key; each group's tab, by its composite's key and the group's title; none until chosen (SHAPE5a0a3b, out of `CompositeView`) |
| `device-view.ts` | `DeviceView`: the device view, what's open in it, by a kind and what of it (`open()`, `'strip:'` and a strip's address), its title and the slot marked (`Opened`); each kind's chain of devices, handed in, the open one's its drawer's to fill it with; what's chosen in the composites it shows, kept as it's opened and closed (`chosen`, SHAPE5a0a3b); closed; whoever listens told as it changes (SHAPE5a0a2, out of the drawer) |
| `transport-lane.ts` | `TransportLane`: the transport as its lane shows it, whether the music plays, and where it is, bar and beat, counted from bar 1 in the clock's meter, or the playhead's place while it's set back; read live, the transport in its own words (SHAPE5a0a) |
| `arrangement/arrangement.ts` | `Arrangement`: what was heard laid out in time, as Ableton's arrangement, in 16ths and rows, the pixels its drawer's: its rows (`rows`), the regions on them, each labelled by its clip, played live or not, greyed once its clip is gone, kept whole past a hold it rang through (`regions`, `PlacedRegion`), the opened tracks' lanes, each on its param's range as the desk declares it, one nothing declares by the fader law (`lanes`, `PlacedLane`); looked at on every beat while it shows (`show()`, `look()`); each frame, the time in view following the present, or the playhead while it plays back, until it's scrolled back (`frame(span)`, `scrollBy()`, `left`, `right`, `following`); a group folded, a track's lanes opened, with shift every one as it goes (`toggleGroup()`, `toggleLanes()`); the playhead placed by a press in the ruler on the nearest beat, its release cueing its time through the function it's handed, then following the playback it starts or jumps (`press()`, `placeAt()`, `release()`, `cue`); where each 16th fell, the clock's `GridMap` (`map`) (SHAPE4a) |
| `arrangement/ports.ts` | What it asks, in its own words, whatever answers fitting by its shape: the recording, its regions and lanes (`Recording`, a MIDI sequencer's recorder); the tracks in groups, a group for a kind played live or played back, each its kind and whether it's yours (since SHAPE5a0d4a, the session view's `Surface` before), a clip's name and whether it's still there (`Tracks`, the control surface that played them); the playhead (`Playhead`, the transport); what the desk declares (`Declared`); the clock (`ArrangementClock`) |
| `arrangement/declared.ts` | `declaredBy()`: what the desk declares, read from its view models: a lane's param, by its strip and its controller there; a strip's controllers in the order the sound goes through it, its inserts' controls, its sends, its volume, pan, mute and solo |
| `arrangement/rows.ts` | Its rows, as the session view groups its tracks: the tracks' groups, each once used, as many tracks as it ever used, folded to its first; the desk's strips with no notes below them, always, from the mixer, each bus with those that go into it, the returns, the master, in the groups the session view labels its sections (`arrangedStrips()`, `ArrangedStrip`, `SectionLabels`; SHAPE5a0d4c, the session view's `start.ts` before); a track's lanes under it once opened, in the order its strip lists them, one it doesn't after; a lane's name, its slot and param, a send by its return (`laneName()`) |
| `arrangement/time-window.ts`, `playhead.ts`, `note-shapes.ts`, `lane-shapes.ts` | The time in view, in 16ths, following a head a bar from the right edge, scrolled back, never before the earliest; where a click in the ruler puts the playhead, on the nearest beat; a region's notes as drawn, their pieces, glides and slides, a note kept whole past a hold; a lane's line, its points played as automation, a glide's curve sampled until it settles |

```js
import { ViewBuilder } from '../libs/view/models/index.ts';
const views = new ViewBuilder({ plan, valueOf, startOf, roleOf, send });   // the tool's port
const utility = views.device(['delay 1', 'utility']);   // its level and pan, its pan's law
const level = utility.controls[0];
level.text;           // '-6.0', in dB
level.turnTo(0.5);    // send({ type: 'param', address: ['delay 1', 'utility', 'level'], value: … })
```
