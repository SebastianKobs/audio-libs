# libs/view/models

View models built from a plan, with no DOM: handed a plan and its values through a port, they hand back what a view shows of it and what it can do, as MVVM's view models do (`mockup/architecture/layout.md`, "How a plan is shown"). Reads only the plan's format and the params' specs, the clock for the arrangement and the clip tracks, and the clip slots' port (`libs/view/control-surface/`), a strip and a mixer on the device view's models (`libs/view/device/models/`); tested in node. Since LAY12b.

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| `mixer-view-builder.ts` | `MixerViewBuilder`: a desk's view models on a device's: a channel as a strip (`strip()`), a desk as a mixer (`mixer()`) |
| `strip.ts` | `Strip`: a channel as a strip, by the channel form's roles: its inserts as devices, its sends' knobs by return, its fader, pan, mute and solo, its level, quiet where its cut is at 0; its name as shown, its label, else its own, a pool's copy its pool's and its number (`shownName()`); its name in little room, its short label, else as shown (`shortName()`, a send's knob, LAY12h1); where it goes, the channel its way out feeds, by that one's name as shown (LAY12c) |
| `mixer.ts` | `Mixer`: a desk as a mixer, its strips by role in the plan's order: its buses, each with the buses that go out into it (`BusGroup`), its returns, its master; a solo click, as Ableton's, alone or with shift added or let go, a pool's copies among them (LAY12c); what strips feed, for a view's lights, each through its way out and the sends said to be up, a pool's copies wired by its rule among them, the master left out (`reached()`, LAY12f) |
| `cards.ts` | `InstrumentCards`: an instrument's patches as the device view edits them, a card for each, its own or a drum rack's pads by their keys, each a preset's readout (`PresetReadout`) on its voice card planned at its preset, its edits sent as the bank's messages (`send`), values from outside over its own (`CardValues`, the tweak panel's knobs), the note's sources set by the note; the patch edited in the bank, not an instrument playing it (SHAPE1a; the tool's since LAY15a). Tested in node on the tests' file of instruments (`test/cards.test.js`) |
| `instrument-racks.ts` | `InstrumentRacks`: the instruments as the device view shows them, read through their readout (`InstrumentsReadout`, `libs/view/control-surface/instruments.ts`: the patch each role plays, a role's cards): a role's instrument a rack of its cards, a pad each by its key and its patch, its status the patch it plays, none with no cards (`InstrumentRack`); its reset, every card of its patches put back as the app's messages; the test note, its pitch and velocity MIDI 1.0's at the edge, the note's own values from their defaults, the starts handed in over them, held from a little after now until it's lifted, pressed and let go as the app's messages (`libs/build/voices/`'s `TestNoteMessage`, SHAPE5b2); which of the note's own values a role's instrument reads, and which instruments read one, for its row to grey the others (SHAPE5a0d4d, the device view's `devices/instruments.ts` before) |
| `clip-tracks.ts` | `ClipTracks`: the clip tracks, as a session view shows them, a clip track for each of a control surface's (`libs/view/control-surface/`): each clip track's clips read from what holds them, a clip player's slots or the surface's own for the clips on none, in the order held, each once, its name, its note count and whether it plays, its holder's to say (`ShownClip`); a clip's ▶ launching it, its × asking first, a second click after a moment and within 3 s deleting it, anything else or 3 s unanswered stopping it asking, through the functions it's handed (SHAPE4c) |
| `placed.ts` | `placedOn()`: where a play goes, its group of tracks and its track, as the tracks' groups say (`groupOf()`): played live, the pool its kind is played on; played back, or a lane, its kind's group; none on no group or no track. The session's and the arrangement's rows alike (`Placed`, ZM1; worked out five times before) |
| `session.ts` | `Session`: what the session view shows of what's played, read in its own words, the MIDI sequencer's stream fitting them by their shape: plays kept per group of tracks, a group as many tracks as its plays ever used, your playing on its kind's pool, a replay on its group (`groupOf()`, handed in); a look at a time (`at()`), each track what holds it and whether it sounded since, or the replay waiting for its first note, each group's clips that sounded; a loop holding its track while it plays, a held note until its note off, by its sender (SHAPE5a0a, the session view's `session-view/session.ts`) |
| `session-tracks.ts` | `SessionTracks`, `SessionGroup`, `SessionTrack`, `TrackNow`: the session's tracks as its mixer shows them, from a look at the session in the control surface's groups (`TracksReadout`): the tracks with notes, in the groups' order, yours marked, a group shown once its first play adds a track; each track named from its group's name and numbered from 1, at its channel's address on the desk (handed in), what it plays now, its clip and a visitor's motif after it, a dash with none, lit while it sounds, waiting while a replay waits for its first note; what sounded since the last look, a replay group's clips and each lit track's channel, for the folded sections' lights (SHAPE5a0d4a, the session view's `draw.ts` before) |
| `toggles.ts` | `Toggles`: keys turned from where each starts, what a view folds or opens by key, a section folded, a group unfolded, a track's lanes opened; one turned alone, or every one handed in as the one clicked goes (SHAPE5a0a3a; the arrangement's `toggled()` before) |
| `views.ts` | `Views`: the session view and the arrangement, which one is open, or none, a tab each opening and closing it, another's closing as this one opens leaving it shown; whoever listens told as it changes (SHAPE5a0a3a, out of the drawer) |
| `transport-lane.ts` | `TransportLane`: the transport as its lane shows it, whether the music plays, and where it is, bar and beat, counted from bar 1 in the clock's meter, or the playhead's place while it's set back; read live, the transport in its own words (SHAPE5a0a) |
| `arrangement/arrangement.ts` | `Arrangement`: what was heard laid out in time, as Ableton's arrangement, in 16ths and rows, the pixels its drawer's: its rows (`rows`), the regions on them, each labelled by its clip, played live or not, greyed once its clip is gone, kept whole past a hold it rang through (`regions`, `PlacedRegion`), the opened tracks' lanes, each on its param's range as the desk declares it, one nothing declares by the fader law (`lanes`, `PlacedLane`); looked at on every beat while it shows (`show()`, `look()`); each frame, the time in view following the present, or the playhead while it plays back, until it's scrolled back (`frame(span)`, `scrollBy()`, `left`, `right`, `following`); a group folded, a track's lanes opened, with shift every one as it goes (`toggleGroup()`, `toggleLanes()`); the playhead placed by a press in the ruler on the nearest beat, its release cueing its time through the function it's handed, then following the playback it starts or jumps (`press()`, `placeAt()`, `release()`, `cue`); where each 16th fell, the clock's `GridMap` (`map`) (SHAPE4a) |
| `arrangement/declared.ts` | `declaredBy()`: what the desk declares, read from its view models: a lane's param, by its strip and its controller there; a strip's controllers in the order the sound goes through it, its inserts' controls, its sends, its volume, pan, mute and solo |
| `arrangement/rows.ts` | Its rows, as the session view groups its tracks: the tracks' groups, each once used, as many tracks as it ever used, folded to its first; the desk's strips with no notes below them, always, from the mixer, each bus with those that go into it, the returns, the master, in the groups the session view labels its sections (`arrangedStrips()`, `ArrangedStrip`, `SectionLabels`; SHAPE5a0d4c, the session view's `start.ts` before); a track's lanes under it once opened, in the order its strip lists them, one it doesn't after; a lane's name, its slot and param, a send by its return (`laneName()`) |
| `arrangement/time-window.ts`, `playhead.ts`, `note-shapes.ts`, `lane-shapes.ts` | The time in view, in 16ths, following a head a bar from the right edge, scrolled back, never before the earliest; where a click in the ruler puts the playhead, on the nearest beat; a region's notes as drawn, their pieces, glides and slides, a note kept whole past a hold; a lane's line, its points played as automation, a glide's curve sampled until it settles |
