# libs/view/instrument/dom

Each role's instrument drawn in the DOM as a device, on a device's drawers (`libs/view/device/dom/`), from the instruments' model (`libs/view/instrument/models/`).

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| `instrument-devices.ts` | `InstrumentDevices`: each role's instrument a device, a chain of the device view's opened by a clip track's roles, drawn from the instruments' model (`InstrumentRacks`) as a synth's panel: its bar, the test note's button held as long as it is, its reset; a drum rack a tab for each pad; the card's face, its groups as its owner titles them, its matrix; its words its owner's (`InstrumentLook`), the test note's row too, drawn in the device view's head and told which instruments are shown each time one is, to grey what none of them reads (SHAPE5a0d4d, the device view's `devices/instruments.ts`) |
