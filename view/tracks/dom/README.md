# libs/view/tracks/dom

The session view's, the clips' and the arrangement's: the view models (`libs/view/tracks/models/`) drawn in the DOM, the desk's drawing (`libs/view/desk/dom/`) and the device view's (`libs/view/device/dom/`): one implementation of the views, as Web Audio is of the sound. The session view's strips are drawn on it too. It imports `libs/params/`, `libs/view/device/dom/`, `libs/view/device/models/`, `libs/view/html/`, `libs/view/desk/dom/`, `libs/view/desk/models/`, `libs/view/tracks/models/` and `libs/view/tracks/readout/`.

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| `clip-tracks-view.ts` | `ClipTracksView`: the clip tracks drawn from their model (`libs/view/tracks/models/clip-tracks.ts`), a section that folds, a column each, made once so it keeps its scroll, its name opening what it opens: a chip a clip, kept with its clip, its name and note count, lit while it sounds, a played track's with ▶, flashing every two beats while it plays, and ×, showing "delete?" while it asks, their hints its owner's (`ClipHints`); a click anywhere in what it's made in handed to its model |
| `arrangement/` (`ArrangementView`, `ArrangementDrawn`) | `ArrangementView`: the arrangement on the page, from its model (`libs/view/tracks/models/arrangement/`): its names beside its time, a group's header folding it, a track's ▸ opening its lanes, with shift every one; a drag or a wheel scrolling it, a press in the ruler placing the playhead, its model cueing it as it's let go; drawn on every frame while it shows, what it drew kept for the browser tests (`drawn()`); `ArrangementDrawn`, what's drawn, as the browser tests read it: the rows, the regions in view and their notes, the opened lanes' lines, whether it follows the present, the place at its left edge, the present's x, and the playhead's place and x where it's set back. Drawn on canvases (`time-drawing.ts`, `TimeDrawing`): the bar ruler, the rows' bands, each region a block labelled with its clip, its notes inside it at their pitch (`note-drawing.ts`), greyed once its clip is gone, a lane's line on its param's range (`lane-drawing.ts`), the present and the playhead; 8 px a 16th (`pixels.ts`); its colors handed in, read as it draws (`ArrangementColors`) |
| `session-drawing.ts` | `SessionDrawing`: when the session view is drawn, by its drawers, from its models: on every beat of the clock while it's shown, the clip tracks told the beat; as it's shown, with what sounded in the last beat, the device view closed as both views hide; playing back, the clock's grid held, a beat after the last drawing; a beat's drawing held while a pointer is pressed on it, so a click lands; the level bars and the device view's knobs on every frame |
| `view-strip.ts` | `ViewStrip`: the strip along the bottom, the session view's sections in one row as the sound flows, then the arrangement, drawn from which one is open (`Views`, `libs/view/tracks/models/views.ts`), shown while either is |

Tested in node on HTML as text and on values, in its `test/` folder: `session-drawing.test.js`, when each beat and frame is drawn; `arrangement.test.js`, the arrangement's x in view and its canvas's pixels.
