# libs/view/tracks/dom

The session view's, the clips' and the arrangement's: the view models (`libs/view/tracks/models/`) drawn in the DOM, the mixer's drawing (`libs/view/mixer/dom/`) and the device view's (`libs/view/device/dom/`): one implementation of the views, as Web Audio is of the sound (`mockup/architecture/layout.md`, "How a plan is shown"). Since LAY12d1, the session view's strips drawn on it.

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| `clip-tracks-view.ts` | `ClipTracksView`: the clip tracks drawn from their model (`libs/view/tracks/models/clip-tracks.ts`), a section that folds, a column each, made once so it keeps its scroll, its name opening what it opens: a chip a clip, kept with its clip, its name and note count, lit while it sounds, a played track's with ▶, flashing every two beats while it plays, and ×, showing "delete?" while it asks; a click anywhere in what it's made in handed to its model (SHAPE4c) |
| `arrangement/` | `ArrangementView`: the arrangement on the page, from its model (`libs/view/tracks/models/arrangement/`): its names beside its time, a group's header folding it, a track's ▸ opening its lanes, with shift every one; a drag or a wheel scrolling it, a press in the ruler placing the playhead, its model cueing it as it's let go; drawn on every frame while it shows, what it drew kept for the browser tests (`drawn()`). Drawn on canvases (`time-drawing.ts`, `TimeDrawing`): the bar ruler, the rows' bands, each region a block labelled with its clip, its notes inside it at their pitch (`note-drawing.ts`), greyed once its clip is gone, a lane's line on its param's range (`lane-drawing.ts`), the present and the playhead; 8 px a 16th (`pixels.ts`); its colors handed in, read as it draws (`ArrangementColors`) (SHAPE4b; `mixer-surface/arrangement/` until then) |
| `session-drawing.ts` | `SessionDrawing`: when the session view is drawn, by its drawers, from its models: on every beat of the clock while it's shown, the clip tracks told the beat; as it's shown, with what sounded in the last beat, the device view closed as both views hide; playing back, the clock's grid held, a beat after the last drawing; a beat's drawing held while a pointer is pressed on it, so a click lands; the level bars and the device view's knobs on every frame (SHAPE5a0d4b, the session view's `draw.ts`) |
| `view-strip.ts` | `ViewStrip`: the strip along the bottom, the session view's sections in one row as the sound flows, then the arrangement, drawn from which one is open (`Views`, `libs/view/tracks/models/views.ts`), shown while either is (SHAPE5a0a, the session view's `views.ts`; its state its model's since SHAPE5a0a3a) |

Tested in node on HTML as text and on values (`test/`); a knob's drag and a value typed, the sections' folding and a group's kept box, the drawing's pointer by the session and device views' end-to-end tests.
