# libs/view/dom

The view models (`libs/view/models/`) drawn in the DOM: one implementation of the views, as Web Audio is of the sound (`mockup/architecture/layout.md`, "How a plan is shown"). Since LAY12d1, the session view's strips drawn on it.

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| `elements.ts` | `makeElement()`, an element made, its class and its text; `html```, HTML made from a template, every value escaped as it's made, a list's items one after another (`Html`); `setHtml()`, the views' one way into the page's HTML, and `appendHtml()`, added after what's there; the session view's until LAY12d1 (`session-view/html.ts`) |
| `bound.ts` | `Bound`: drawn elements bound to their models, each element marked with its model's address bound, as it's drawn, by whoever drew it from its models, to the model there, so a drag, a refresh and a click find it by the element alone, with no routing; `CONTROL_MARKS`, the marks a control is drawn with, a knob, a live one's drag, a switch, a list of choices (SHAPE4d1) |
| `knob-view.ts` | `knobHtml()`: a knob drawn, its label, its bar by its taper, a gain's unity marked, a pan's from its middle, a ring under it for each source that moves it (LAY15a), its text, marked with its address (`data-knob`); a live one marked for its drag and its hint (`data-set`), a read-only one by how it's set, empty where nothing sets it now; `refreshKnobs()`, the knobs within an element brought up to date as they play; `stillPanHtml()`, a pan that does nothing, on a strip whose channel has none; `faderHtml()`, a fader, upright, its unity marked; `KnobDrag`, the live knobs within an element dragged, up or down, the whole travel in 200 px, a tenth of that with shift, a setting set once as the drag ends, typed into by a click on its value, Enter setting it, one it can't read kept marked (LAY15a), and set back to their start by a double-click; each found by the element it's drawn as, bound to its model as it was drawn (`bound.ts`, SHAPE4d1) |
| `meter-view.ts` | `meterPosition()`, where a level shows on a level bar, even in dB, -18 dBFS two thirds up; `meterHtml()`, a level bar by the name its level is read by, red at full scale; `mixer-surface/meters.ts` until LAY12d1; its fall, and each bar's level kept by its key, a model's since SHAPE5a0a3c (`meterFall()`, `Meters`, `libs/view/models/meters.ts`, `Levels` here before) |
| `device-view.ts` | `deviceHtml()`: a device drawn from its model (LAY12e1), its bar its status and its reset, then its controls (`controlsHtml()`, a group's too), a view of its own first, each its name and unit, its hint as the view gives it, a knob, a switch or a choice; a setting shown only where it's live; a time with a sync switch its note while synced, its free time while not (LAY12e2, `DeviceLook`) |
| `group-view.ts` | `groupHtml()`: a group of a composite's children drawn, as a synth's panel groups its modules: its title, a tab for each child where it has more than one (`data-tab`), the chosen one's controls, a view of its own first (`Group`, `GroupLook`, LAY15a) |
| `composite-view.ts` | `CompositeView`: a composite drawn in a device's box, an instrument's and a strip's alike, as its look says (`CompositeLook`): with a face, its own device as a group, its children grouped by role in tabs in the look's order, its matrix; or flat, its controls in a device of its own, as a strip's insert is; a rack of composites a pad each, the chosen one's box shown (`Rack`); its bar its status, its owner's buttons and a reset where its look gives one; the chosen pad of each rack and tab of each group its model's, handed in (`Chosen`, SHAPE5a0a3b), each reset its own, a click within what it drew choosing or resetting; drawn into an element, each control bound to its model (SHAPE4d2); each control's hint in one wording, each device's view of its own registered by its kind and redrawn on every frame while it's shown (SHAPE4d3) |
| `hint.ts` | `controlHint()`: a control's hint, one wording for every view, worded from the control alone: its own words; how it's set, live a double-click bringing it back, by its macro, by its notes its automation recorded in the arrangement's lanes, fixed as the desk built it, else by its source, read-only; its modulators, its ring showing how far (SHAPE4d3, the instruments' and the inserts' merged) |
| `custom-view.ts` | `CustomView`: a view of its own, the contract every one meets, registered by a device's kind: drawn before its device's controls as HTML (`html()`), and once in the page, handed the element its device's controls are drawn in, what redraws it on every frame while it's shown, false once it's gone (`shown()`); the envelope's curve and the limiter's history (SHAPE4d3) |
| `envelope-view.ts` | `envelopeHtml()`: an envelope's curve drawn as SVG, its line and the area under it, its delay's end dotted, the note's end dashed, a second 60 px across, so a long one runs on (LAY15a); `EnvelopeView`, an envelope generator's view of its own, its curve as it's played, drawn again on a frame its knobs changed it (SHAPE4d3) |
| `matrix-view.ts` | `matrixHtml()`: a matrix drawn, a row a source and what it moves, how much: a macro's map as text, its factor, offset and ends; a modulator's reach a knob of its own (LAY15a) |
| `choice-view.ts` | `choiceHtml()`: a choice drawn as a list of its options, the one it is chosen, closed where it isn't live; `switchHtml()`: a switch on or off, a click toggling a live one; each marked with its address (LAY12e2) |
| `limiter-view.ts` | `LimiterView`: the limiter's view of its own, registered for its kind: its history, its columns a model's since SHAPE5a0a3c (`LimiterHistory`), what came out and in red what it cut, on the meters' scale, its ceiling a line, and what it takes away now, read from the limiter block inside the device by its address (LAY12f, from the session view's `devices/limiter.ts`); a view of its own as every one is, its history and its reading drawn on every frame once shown (SHAPE4d3) |
| `section-view.ts` | `SectionView`: a section of a view, its label and its strips in a row; one that folds, its label an arrow and a dot, folded to its first strip by a click on its label, with shift every section beside it (LAY12h2), which are folded a model's (`Toggles`), by each section's label, shared by every section beside it, drawn from it (SHAPE5a0a3a), its light on while a hidden strip sounds, by what it sounds as (`data-sounds`) (LAY12h1, the session view's `sections.ts`) |
| `mixer-view.ts` | `MixerView`: a mixer drawn in its sections as the sound flows, the tracks, the buses, the FX busses and the master: the groups of tracks the view hands it (`MixerGroup`, `MixerTrack`), each in a box kept across drawings, its scroll and its width in tracks, folded to its first track until it's unfolded, which a model's (`Toggles`, SHAPE5a0a3a); the desk's own channels from the mixer model (`deskHtml()`), a bus with those that go into it in a box; a track's strip, empty while it plays nothing (`trackHtml()`); a strip's devices opened by its address (`data-open`), its sends labelled by their returns' short labels; the level bars on every frame; the lights, by what the strips heard feed (`fedBy()`) (LAY12h1, the session view's `strips.ts` and `draw.ts`); its clicks its own, a strip's name or slot told by its address (`onOpen()`), mute, solo with shift, a group's fold, its knobs' drag (LAY12h2, the session view's `clicks.ts`) |
| `clip-tracks-view.ts` | `ClipTracksView`: the clip tracks drawn from their model (`libs/view/models/clip-tracks.ts`), a section that folds, a column each, made once so it keeps its scroll, its name opening what it opens: a chip a clip, kept with its clip, its name and note count, lit while it sounds, a played track's with ▶, flashing every two beats while it plays, and ×, showing "delete?" while it asks; a click anywhere in what it's made in handed to its model (SHAPE4c) |
| `canvas.ts` | `sizedCanvas()`: a canvas sized for the screen's pixels, as many as it has to a CSS pixel, its context drawing in CSS pixels, as wide as it's shown where it's given no width; the arrangement's and the limiter's history's (SHAPE4b) |
| `arrangement/` | `ArrangementView`: the arrangement on the page, from its model (`libs/view/models/arrangement/`): its names beside its time, a group's header folding it, a track's ▸ opening its lanes, with shift every one; a drag or a wheel scrolling it, a press in the ruler placing the playhead, its model cueing it as it's let go; drawn on every frame while it shows, what it drew kept for the browser tests (`drawn()`). Drawn on canvases (`time-drawing.ts`, `TimeDrawing`): the bar ruler, the rows' bands, each region a block labelled with its clip, its notes inside it at their pitch (`note-drawing.ts`), greyed once its clip is gone, a lane's line on its param's range (`lane-drawing.ts`), the present and the playhead; 8 px a 16th (`pixels.ts`); its colors handed in, read as it draws (`ArrangementColors`) (SHAPE4b; `mixer-surface/arrangement/` until then) |
| `device-view-panel.ts` | `DeviceViewPanel`: the device view drawn from its model (`libs/view/models/device-view.ts`), as Ableton's detail view, below the tracks, a box's devices left to right, drawn again as its model changes: each kind of opening a chain of devices (`DeviceChain`), filling its row and, where it has one, its own part of the head, shown while its kind is open (an instrument's test note); a slot's device marked and scrolled to; its knobs dragged, toggled and chosen, each by the element it's drawn as, drawn again so what it shows follows, and brought up to date with the views of their own on every frame (SHAPE5a0a, the session view's `session-view/device-view.ts`; its state its model's since SHAPE5a0a2) |
| `insert-devices.ts` | `InsertDevices`: a strip's inserts, the master's too, a chain of the device view's, opened by the strip's address: each insert its owner says something of a composite drawn flat, in the order its sound goes through them, its status its label, its job and what sets it, a reset for what's live on it (SHAPE5a0a, the session view's `devices/inserts.ts`) |
| `transport-lane-view.ts` | `TransportLaneView`: the transport's lane drawn from its model (`libs/view/models/transport-lane.ts`) at the top of the element it's handed, ▶ lit while it plays, ■, and where the music is, bar and beat, written only as it changes; ▶ and ■ sent as messages, play and stop; its hints its owner's (`TransportHints`) (SHAPE5a0a, the session view's `transport-lane.ts`) |
| `view-strip.ts` | `ViewStrip`: the strip along the bottom, the session view's sections in one row as the sound flows, then the arrangement, drawn from which one is open (`Views`, `libs/view/models/views.ts`), shown while either is (SHAPE5a0a, the session view's `views.ts`; its state its model's since SHAPE5a0a3a) |
| `strip-view.ts` | `stripHtml()`: a strip drawn from its model, its name, where it goes and what plays on it as the view says (`StripLook`), its slots, its sends, pan, fader, level bar, value, mute and solo; quiet where it's silent; `slotsHtml()`, its inserts' slots, each its device and what sets it; `muteSoloHtml()`, its mute and solo by its key |

Tested in node on HTML as text and on values (`test/`); a knob's drag and a value typed, the sections' folding and a group's kept box by the session and device views' end-to-end tests.
