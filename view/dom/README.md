# libs/view/dom

The view models (`libs/view/models/`) drawn in the DOM: one implementation of the views, as Web Audio is of the sound (`mockup/architecture/layout.md`, "How a plan is shown"). Since LAY12d1, the session view's strips drawn on it.

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| `elements.ts` | `makeElement()`, an element made, its class and its text; `html```, HTML made from a template, every value escaped as it's made, a list's items one after another (`Html`); `setHtml()`, the views' one way into the page's HTML, and `appendHtml()`, added after what's there; the session view's until LAY12d1 (`session-view/html.ts`) |
| `knob-view.ts` | `knobHtml()`: a knob drawn, its label, its bar by its taper, a gain's unity marked, a pan's from its middle, a ring under it for each source that moves it (LAY15a), its text, marked with its address (`data-knob`); a live one marked for its drag and its hint (`data-set`), a read-only one by how it's set, empty where nothing sets it now; `refreshKnobs()`, the knobs within an element brought up to date as they play; `stillPanHtml()`, a pan that does nothing, on a strip whose channel has none; `faderHtml()`, a fader, upright, its unity marked; `KnobDrag`, the live knobs within an element dragged, up or down, the whole travel in 200 px, a tenth of that with shift, a setting set once as the drag ends, typed into by a click on its value, Enter setting it, one it can't read kept marked (LAY15a), and set back to their start by a double-click; each found by its address and the element it's drawn as, which says whose it is |
| `meter-view.ts` | `meterFall()`, a level bar's fall, as a DIN peak meter's; `meterPosition()`, where a level shows on it, even in dB, -18 dBFS two thirds up; `meterHtml()`, a level bar by the name its level is read by, red at full scale; `mixer-surface/meters.ts` until LAY12d1; `Levels`, each bar's level kept by its key, falling (LAY12h1) |
| `device-view.ts` | `deviceHtml()`: a device drawn from its model (LAY12e1), its bar its status and its reset, then its controls (`controlsHtml()`, a group's too), a view of its own first, each its name and unit, its hint as the view gives it, a knob, a switch or a choice; a setting shown only where it's live; a time with a sync switch its note while synced, its free time while not (LAY12e2, `DeviceLook`) |
| `group-view.ts` | `groupHtml()`: a group of a composite's children drawn, as a synth's panel groups its modules: its title, a tab for each child where it has more than one (`data-tab`), the chosen one's controls, a view of its own first (`Group`, `GroupLook`, LAY15a) |
| `envelope-view.ts` | `envelopeHtml()`: an envelope's curve drawn as SVG, its line and the area under it, its delay's end dotted, the note's end dashed, a second 60 px across, so a long one runs on (LAY15a) |
| `matrix-view.ts` | `matrixHtml()`: a matrix drawn, a row a source and what it moves, how much: a macro's map as text, its factor, offset and ends; a modulator's reach a knob of its own (LAY15a) |
| `choice-view.ts` | `choiceHtml()`: a choice drawn as a list of its options, the one it is chosen, closed where it isn't live; `switchHtml()`: a switch on or off, a click toggling a live one; each marked with its address (LAY12e2) |
| `limiter-view.ts` | `LimiterView`: the limiter's view of its own, registered for its kind: its history, what came out and in red what it cut, on the meters' scale, its ceiling a line, and what it takes away now, read from the limiter block inside the device by its address (LAY12f, from the session view's `devices/limiter.ts`) |
| `section-view.ts` | `SectionView`: a section of a view, its label and its strips in a row; one that folds, its label an arrow and a dot, folded to its first strip by a click on its label, with shift every section beside it (LAY12h2), its light on while a hidden strip sounds, by what it sounds as (`data-sounds`) (LAY12h1, the session view's `sections.ts`) |
| `mixer-view.ts` | `MixerView`: a mixer drawn in its sections as the sound flows, the tracks, the buses, the FX busses and the master: the groups of tracks the view hands it (`MixerGroup`, `MixerTrack`), each in a box kept across drawings, its scroll and its width in tracks; the desk's own channels from the mixer model (`deskHtml()`), a bus with those that go into it in a box; a track's strip, empty while it plays nothing (`trackHtml()`); a strip's devices opened by its address (`data-open`), its sends labelled by their returns' short labels; the level bars on every frame; the lights, by what the strips heard feed (`fedBy()`) (LAY12h1, the session view's `strips.ts` and `draw.ts`); its clicks its own, a strip's name or slot told by its address (`onOpen()`), mute, solo with shift, a group's fold, its knobs' drag (LAY12h2, the session view's `clicks.ts`) |
| `strip-view.ts` | `stripHtml()`: a strip drawn from its model, its name, where it goes and what plays on it as the view says (`StripLook`), its slots, its sends, pan, fader, level bar, value, mute and solo; quiet where it's silent; `slotsHtml()`, its inserts' slots, each its device and what sets it; `muteSoloHtml()`, its mute and solo by its key |

Tested in node on HTML as text and on values (`test/`); a knob's drag and a value typed, the sections' folding and a group's kept box by the session and device views' end-to-end tests.
