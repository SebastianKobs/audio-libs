# web-audio-mixer

The desk every sound goes through, in the engine's context, laid out as on a mixing desk: a bigger mix gets fuller, never louder, and nothing starts at full volume. A package of its own since X4, moved out of `sound/`, on the routing, the engine and the FX chains (`libs/mixer/`, `libs/web-audio-engine/`, `libs/web-audio-fx/`); it makes no node itself, but connects the nodes the engine built, so another engine needs its own.

```
channel ─ fader ─┬──────────────────────────────────────────────▶ master (or a bus)
                 └─▶ send ─────────────▶ FX channel's input
bus (a submix) ─ fader ───────────────────────────────────────────▶ master
FX channel: input ─▶ chain of FX modules ─▶ level ─▶ pan ─┬─▶ master (or a bus)
                                                          └─▶ sends ─▶ other FX channels' inputs
master: the sum ─▶ its insert (the limiter, built by the caller) ─▶ master volume ─▶ output
```

```ts
import { Mixer } from './index.ts';
const mixer = new Mixer(engine, { volume: 0.6, master: limiter });   // the master starts at 0 and fades in; limiter built by the caller
const reverb = mixer.fx('reverb', { volume: 0.6 });   // build its chain (libs/web-audio-fx/) between reverb.input and reverb.level
const tap = mixer.channel('tap', { volume: 0.7, sends: ['reverb'] });
const sings = mixer.bus('sings', { volume: 1 });
const echo = mixer.fx('delay 1', { volume: 0.8, pan: -0.45, sends: [{ to: 'reverb', amount: 0.4 }], out: 'sings' });
tap.volume(0.5);                                       // glides the fader
```

| | |
|---|---|
| `new Mixer(engine, { volume, master })` | the sum, its insert, the master, into the context's output. The master fades in from 0 (0.25 s). `master`, a `MasterInsert` the caller built, goes between the sum and the master volume: its `input` and `output`, the `points` a meter reads inside it by name, and its `readings` by name. The desk's (`Desk`) is the master's insert slots, today the Limiter module (`LIMITER`, `libs/web-audio-fx/`), as Ableton's on its master: its gain into it, in dB, held at its ceiling, in dBFS, its release in s. Without one, the sum goes straight into the master |
| `reading(name)` | a reading the master's insert offers, by name, now: what its limiter takes away, in dB (`'limiter reduction'`); 0 with none, or one it doesn't offer |
| `channel(name, { volume, pan, sends, out, inserts })` | a `Channel`: a fader, out to the master (`'master'`) or a bus, sent after the fader to the FX channels named, at 1. With `pan`, a stereo panner after the fader, its sends and its way out after it, so a mono sound is as loud in the middle (`panTo(v)`). `input`, `volume(v)`. With `inserts` ({ name, input, output }, built by the caller), its input runs through each, in order, into its `fader`. A send written `{ to, amount, from }` has a gain of its own (`sendTo(to, v)` glides it), taken after the fader, before it (`from: 'pre'`, after the inserts), or at an insert's way in (`from:` its name). Its fader and each send with a gain handed out as stages, the Gain modules they are (`faderStage`, `sendStages`), for what sets them from outside: a track's controllers, at their time, among them where its notes are heard from (`libs/spatial-audio/`'s values, F6b, H3) |
| `bus(name, { volume, pan, sends, out, inserts })` | a `Channel` of the kind `'bus'`, a submix: a channel whose input takes what's routed into it, channels, FX channels and anything played into it. With sends, a group, as Ableton's: its tracks routed into it, through its fader, sent after it |
| `fx(name, { volume, pan, sends, out, inserts })` | an `FxChannel`, its options and its sends a channel's, on one `ChannelStrip` with it (`strip.ts`, DRY8b), its volume its `level`, stereo: `input`, where sends arrive; a chain of FX modules, built by the caller between `input` and `chainOut`: its `inserts`, as a channel's but after the chain (a Utility at the end, as an Ableton chain ends), in order, into its `level`, or else its `level`; its `level`; a `pan`, if it has one; `sends` to other FX channels, by name or `{ to, amount, from }` as a channel's, taken after the pan, so they keep its panning, or from where they say; out to the master or a bus. `volume(v)`, `panTo(v)` and `sendTo(name, v)` glide its level, its pan and a send; its sends handed out as stages, as a channel's (`sendStages`) |
| `peak(name)` | a strip's peak, as a meter reads it: the loudest sample of the last 1024, left or right, after its fader and pan; `'master'` after its insert and its volume; a point its insert names, inside it: the desk's limiter's `'limiter in'` after its gain, `'limiter out'` after it, before the master volume. Its analysers are made at its first read, so a desk nobody looks at has none |
| `strips` | the desk, by name: each a `Strip`, a `Channel` or an `FxChannel` |
| `setOn(strip, key, value, timing, slotted)`, `timingOf(change, { now, at })` | a change set on a built strip by its parameter's key (`libs/desk-plan/`'s `ParamKey`), by a timing (`libs/web-audio-fx/`'s `Timing`): its own volume or pan, or a send by its return, through the strip's own `set(control, value, timing)`, a send built as it's first turned up; or a slot's chain at the port its device declares (`slotted(slot)`, its chain and ports); false for a mute or a solo, which the routing keeps, and where nothing's built. When a change lands: gliding by its time constant, from its time where that's to come; at its time on the audio's clock; else now (DRY9c) (`target.ts`) |
| `new TrackStrips({ on, ports, now, templateOf, made, setByNotes })` | a track's strip, made the first time it's asked for (`stripOf(track)`), as its first note or change comes, and kept (`of(track)`): built from the template its owner hands over (`templateOf()`, none for a track with no strip here) at its values, as its owner knows it (`made()`); a change by its key, its value, its time and maybe a glide, set through its target where the track's notes set it (`setByNotes()`), now, at its time on the audio's clock or gliding; false for one it doesn't set (`track-strips.ts`) |
| `new Desk({ desk, master, devices, modules, moduleOf, params, beat, valuesOf, built, volume })` | a whole desk built from data (`desk.ts`): its `routing` from its strips' data as it's made, before any sound; as the audio starts (`build(engine)`), the master's insert slots from its data (`master`, a strip's, any number), a chain in their order between the sum and the master volume, their points and readings named by slot (`'limiter in'`, `'limiter reduction'`), the mixer on the routing with them, its volume its owner's (`volume()`), each strip in its data's order at the values its owner hands over (`valuesOf()`), a slot whose device is among the device params (`libs/web-audio-fx/`'s `DeviceParams`, by `deviceAt()`) at its params as they play and set through them, each FX channel's chain of modules as they play (`FxModules`, `moduleOf()` naming each effect's), its own params among the device params; each strip's chains kept (`slots`, by strip and slot) and handed back (`built()`); `bus`, each bus's way in; `chains`, each FX channel's chain; `tempo(beat)`, the chains following the tempo |
| `addLiveDevices(params, { desk, master, devices, ports, ruled, effects })` | a desk's slots as live devices among the device params (`live-devices.ts`), each named by its strip and its slot: every slot of its strips and its master but those its owner's rules set (`ruled()`), its start its device's module's params with the slot's own over them, its ports its device's; every FX channel's chain's module with params of its own one too, its params' specs its effect's (`effects()`) |
| `new DeskChanges({ desk, mix, params, clock, at, lead })` | changes to a desk's params by their keys (`desk-changes.ts`): each checked as it comes, a key the desk's strip can't have thrown (the master's pan or a send, a send to what's no return, a slot with no device); held (`libs/desk-plan/`'s `HeldChanges`), one that can glide until its `lead` ahead of its time, then set on the audio at its time (`timingOf()`, `at()` the audio's clock), a switch and a param that rebuilds its device until its time (DRY9c); set by the key's shape, a strip's own volume or pan and a send on the live mix (`LiveMix`, by its key and a timing), mute and solo its switches, a slot's param on its device among the device params |
| `volume(v)` | glides the master (0.05 s) |
| `pause()`, `resume()` | the master fades out (0.08 s), and the context stops 0.4 s later. Back before it stopped, it fades in from where it got to; stopped, from 0. Never at full volume |
| `sum`, `master` | its nodes |

Where each strip goes is its `routing`, a `Routing` (`libs/mixer/`, since X3b; drawn out of the mixer in X3a), its own, or its owner's, handed in (`new Mixer(engine, { routing })`, Z3), so what's on the desk is known before there's any sound; a strip already on it is built where it says, a way out asked or not, what's asked agreeing or it throws, and one not on it is put on it as asked. The mixer asks it before it wires: a strip's kind, `channel`, `bus` or `return` (an FX channel), its way out (`outOf(name)`) and what it sends to (`sentTo(name)`). A send or an output goes only to what's on the desk already, so there are no loops; anything else throws. `route(name, out)` moves a strip's output while it plays, to the master or a bus, and refuses one that feeds it, through an output or a send. Every strip has a mute, `muted(on)`: a channel's or bus's at its input, so its sends go quiet too, an FX channel's at its output, after its pan, so its own tail does. `routing.silenced(muted, soloed)` says which strips are silent, as Ableton's mute and solo: a solo keeps its way out and what feeds it, and never silences a return. The mixer keeps them (`mutes(muted, soloed)`, since Z4), each strip silenced as the routing says, now, and one made later as it's made, a name not built yet waiting for it; `silent`, the strips silent now. Tested in node on the engine's stand-in for Web Audio (`test/mixer.test.js`, out of `engine.test.js` in W3b, in its `test/` folder since X4); the routing on its own, with no signal, in its library (`libs/mixer/test/routing.test.js`).
