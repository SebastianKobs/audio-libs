# music

What the music knows, apart from its time and its sound. Part of [the libraries](../README.md).

- [`music-theory/`](music-theory/README.md): notes, keys, the standard scales and chords, a play style.
- [`phrasing/`](phrasing/README.md): a phrase played as a player would.

Reads: `libs/base/util/`; `phrasing/` also `libs/sequencing/` (`clock`, `sequencer`, `midi-sequencer`).
