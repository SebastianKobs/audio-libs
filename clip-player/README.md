# clip-player

The clip player, its model, as Ableton's session view holds and plays clips: tracks of clip slots, each slot holding a clip, a pattern of notes; a clip launched from its slot plays on its track, one a track, looping on the grid, or once from the next beat, its first note to its last, on its own track or on one its owner lends it as it starts, its notes sent into the input a control surface plays into (`libs/midi-sequencer/`), so they reach their instrument and the stream as a played note does. A clip's notes are pitches, or steps of the scale pitched in the key as it's launched, so a clip follows a change of key the next time it's launched. It imports the clock, the sequencer, MIDI, MIDI's sequencer and music theory. No DOM: its port and its view model are the views' (`libs/view/`).

```ts
import { ClipPlayer } from './index.ts';
const clips = new ClipPlayer({ input: sequencer, grid: clock.grid, key: () => key() });
clips.put(bassSlot, { to: { track: 'bass', clip: bassSlot, live: false }, pattern: BASS, transpose: -12, straight: true });
clips.launch(bassSlot);                  // pitched in the key now, looping on its track from the next 16th
clock.onStep((i, t) => clips.step(i, t)); // every playing clip's notes due in the 16th i sent on
```

| | |
|---|---|
| `new ClipPlayer({ input, grid, key })`, `step(i, t)` | the clips playing, one a track: each 16th, every playing clip's notes due there sent on, in the order the clips were launched, each with its note on and its note off together, its length known as it starts, a fresh index each, the player one source (note rotation); the looping and when each note comes due the sequencer's clip launcher's (`libs/sequencer/`, `ClipLauncher`); `key()`, the key a clip's steps are pitched in, read as it's launched (`player.ts`) |
| `Clip`, `ClipNote` | a clip: where its notes go (`to`, an address on the MIDI sequencer's input), its pattern of notes, each a `pitch` or a `step` of the scale, its `part`, its per-note `values` as it starts and a held note's `glides`, each `[ticks after its start, pitch]`, sent as MIDI 2.0's per-note controllers; `transpose`, semitones once its notes are pitched, its glides with them; `straight`, under the grid's swing or on it, a note's own before it; `velocity()`, read as each note comes due, as a velocity device sets it, full if it has none, none leaving the note out; `once`, played once from the next beat; `lend(to, span)`, for a clip played once, the track its owner lends it as it starts, for its span, handed its own address, its track the one it borrows from (`player.ts`) |
| `put(slot, clip)`, `remove(slot)`, `slotsOn(track)`, `clipIn(slot)` | the slots: a clip put in its slot, in place of the one it held, a slot known by whatever its owner gives; a slot emptied, its clip stopped where it plays; a track's slots in the order they were first put, a slot's track the one its clip's notes go to, or borrow from; a slot's clip, none where it's empty (`player.ts`) |
| `ClipMessage<Slot>` | what's asked of a clip in its slot, as a session view's clip asks, as a bus carries it (`libs/util/`'s `Bus`): `clip launched`, its slot's clip played on its track; `clip deleted`, its slot's clip gone, its owner's to say what goes with it (`messages.ts`) |
| `launch(slot, from?)`, `stop(track)` | a slot's clip launched on its track, in place of what the track played, pitched in the key then, its notes carrying a fresh region, one while it loops, as the recorder tells one launch from another; a lent clip one a slot, launched again from the next beat on a track lent again; an empty slot launches nothing; launched `from` a 16th not stepped yet, as Ableton's launch quantization has it, the clip waits and is launched as that 16th comes, before it plays, pitched in the key it was asked in, what its track plays playing on till then, a later launch on the track in its place, a stop or an emptied slot letting it go; a track stopped (`player.ts`) |
| `playing(slot)` | whether a slot's clip plays, as of the last 16th: one played once from its start until its last note ends, or as long as its track is lent, its ring-out among it, a looping one from its launch until it's stopped, a clip still queued not yet; as a view's clip tracks read it, the player fitting their port by its shape (`player.ts`) |

Tested in node, in its `test/` folder (`test/player.test.js`): a launched clip's notes as they come due, round its loop, on the swing or straight, at its velocity; one clip a track; a launch's region; the slots, and one emptied; a clip's steps pitched in the key it's launched in, transposed; a note's part, values and glides; a clip played once on a lent track; whether a slot's clip plays, a lent one as long as its track is lent; a launch queued for a 16th to come, replaced, let go by a stop or an emptied slot, pitched in the key it was asked in.
