# libs/modular-audio/view/device/dom

A plan's devices drawn in the DOM from their view models (`libs/modular-audio/view/device/models/`), on the views' HTML (`libs/base/html/`): one implementation of the views, as Web Audio is of the sound. It imports `libs/base/params/`, `libs/modular-audio/plan/format/`, `libs/base/util/`, `libs/base/html/` and the models it draws.

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| `bound.ts` | `Bound`: drawn elements bound to their models, each element marked with its model's address bound, as it's drawn, by whoever drew it from its models, to the model there, so a drag, a refresh and a click find it by the element alone, with no routing; `CONTROL_MARKS`, the marks a control is drawn with, a knob, a live one's drag, a switch, a list of choices |
| `knob-view.ts` | `knobHtml()`: a knob drawn, its label, its bar by its taper, a gain's unity marked, a pan's from its middle, a ring under it for each source that moves it, its text, marked with its address (`data-knob`); a live one marked for its drag and its hint (`data-set`), a read-only one by how it's set, empty where nothing sets it now; `refreshKnobs()`, the knobs within an element brought up to date as they play; `stillPanHtml()`, a pan that does nothing, on a strip whose channel has none; `faderHtml()`, a fader, upright, its unity marked; `KnobDrag`, the live knobs within an element dragged, up or down, the whole travel in 200 px, a tenth of that with shift, a setting set once as the drag ends, typed into by a click on its value, Enter setting it, one it can't read kept marked, and set back to their start by a double-click; each found by the element it's drawn as, bound to its model as it was drawn (`bound.ts`) |
| `EMPTY` | a value nothing sets now, as a knob or a fader shows it (`knob-view.ts`) |
| `meter-view.ts` | `meterPosition()`, where a level shows on a level bar, even in dB, -18 dBFS two thirds up; `meterHtml()`, a level bar by the name its level is read by, red at full scale; its fall, and each bar's level kept by its key, are the models' (`meterFall()`, `Meters`, `libs/modular-audio/view/device/models/meters.ts`) |
| `device-view.ts` | `deviceHtml()`: a device drawn from its model, its bar its status and its reset, then its controls (`controlsHtml()`, a group's too), a view of its own first, each its name and unit, its hint as the view gives it, a knob, a switch or a choice; a setting shown only where it's live; a time with a sync switch its note while synced, its free time while not (`DeviceLook`) |
| `group-view.ts` | `groupHtml()`: a group of a composite's children drawn, as a synth's panel groups its modules: its title, a tab for each child where it has more than one (`data-tab`), the chosen one's controls, a view of its own first (`Group`, `GroupLook`) |
| `composite-view.ts` (`Composite`) | `Composite`: a composite as a view shows it, what its chosen tabs are kept by, its models, its address. `CompositeView`: a composite drawn in a device's box, an instrument's and a strip's alike, as its look says (`CompositeLook`): with a face, its own device as a group, its children grouped by role in tabs in the look's order, its matrix; or flat, its controls in a device of its own, as a strip's insert is; a rack of composites a pad each, the chosen one's box shown (`Rack`); its bar its status, its owner's buttons and a reset where its look gives one; the chosen pad of each rack and tab of each group its model's, handed in (`Chosen`), each reset its own, a click within what it drew choosing or resetting; drawn into an element, each control bound to its model; each control's hint in one wording, each device's view of its own registered by its kind and redrawn on every frame while it's shown |
| `hint.ts` | `controlHint()`: a control's hint, one wording for every view, worded from the control alone: its own words; how it's set, live a double-click bringing it back, by its macro, by its notes its automation recorded in lanes of a recording, fixed as the desk built it, else by its source, read-only; its modulators, its ring showing how far |
| `custom-view.ts` | `CustomView`: a view of its own, the contract every one meets, registered by a device's kind: drawn before its device's controls as HTML (`html()`), and once in the page, handed the element its device's controls are drawn in, what redraws it on every frame while it's shown, false once it's gone (`shown()`); the envelope's curve and the limiter's history |
| `envelope-view.ts` | `envelopeHtml()`: an envelope's curve drawn as SVG, its line and the area under it, its delay's end dotted, the note's end dashed, a second 60 px across, so a long one runs on; `EnvelopeView`, an envelope generator's view of its own, its curve as it's played, drawn again on a frame its knobs changed it |
| `matrix-view.ts` | `matrixHtml()`: a matrix drawn, a row a source and what it moves, how much: a macro's map as text, its factor, offset and ends; a modulator's reach a knob of its own |
| `choice-view.ts` | `choiceHtml()`: a choice drawn as a list of its options, the one it is chosen, closed where it isn't live; `switchHtml()`: a switch on or off, a click toggling a live one; each marked with its address |
| `limiter-view.ts` (`LimiterLook`) | `LimiterView`: the limiter's view of its own, registered for its kind: its history, its columns a model's (`LimiterHistory`), what came out and in red what it cut, on the meters' scale, its ceiling a line, and what it takes away now, read from the limiter block inside the device by its address; a view of its own as every one is, its history and its reading drawn on every frame once shown. `LimiterLook`: what it's drawn with, the view's: the colors of what comes out and of its ceiling; this frame's step in seconds, what its history scrolls by |
| `device-panel-view.ts` | `DevicePanelView`: the device view drawn from its model (`DevicePanel`, `libs/modular-audio/view/device/models/device-panel.ts`), as Ableton's detail view, below the tracks, a box's devices left to right, drawn again as its model changes: each kind of opening a chain of devices (`DeviceChain`), filling its row and, where it has one, its own part of the head, shown while its kind is open (an instrument's test note); a slot's device marked and scrolled to; its knobs dragged, toggled and chosen, each by the element it's drawn as, drawn again so what it shows follows, and brought up to date with the views of their own on every frame |

Tested in node, in its `test/` folder: `bound.test.js`, `composite-view.test.js`, `device-views.test.js`, `envelope-view.test.js`, `group-view.test.js`, `hint.test.js`, `knob-view.test.js`, `matrix-view.test.js`, `meter-view.test.js`.
