# libs/modular-audio/view/desk/dom

A desk drawn in the DOM, on a device's drawers (`libs/modular-audio/view/device/dom/`). It imports `libs/base/params/`, `libs/base/util/`, `libs/base/html/`, the device models and drawers it draws with, and the desk's view models (`libs/modular-audio/view/desk/models/`).

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| `section-view.ts` | `SectionView`: a section of a view, its label and its strips in a row; one that folds, its label an arrow and a dot, folded to its first strip by a click on its label, with shift every section beside it, which are folded a model's (`Toggles`), by each section's label, shared by every section beside it, drawn from it, its light on while a hidden strip sounds, by what it sounds as (`data-sounds`) |
| `desk-view.ts`, `desk-html.ts` (`DeskLook`) | `DeskView`: a desk drawn in its sections as the sound flows, the tracks, the buses, the FX busses and the master: the groups of tracks the view hands it (`DeskGroup` and `DeskTrack`), the desk's own channels as one where it's handed none, a track's cell drawn from what it plays now, lit while it sounds, dimmed while it waits, each in a box kept across drawings, its scroll and its width in tracks, folded to its first track until it's unfolded, which a model's (`Toggles`); the desk's own channels from the desk model (`deskHtml()`), a bus with those that go into it in a box; a track's strip, empty while it plays nothing (`trackHtml()`); a strip's devices opened by its address (`data-open`), its sends labelled by their returns' short labels; the level bars on every frame; the lights, by what the strips heard feed (`fedBy()`); its clicks its own, a strip's name or slot told by its address (`onOpen()`), mute, solo with shift, a group's fold, its knobs' drag (its HTML in `desk-html.ts`). `DeskLook`: how a view shows its desk: each section's label; how many tracks a group's box shows before it scrolls, 0 shows all |
| `insert-devices.ts` | `InsertDevices`: a strip's inserts, the master's too, a chain of the device view's, opened by the strip's address: each insert its owner says something of a composite drawn flat, in the order its sound goes through them, its status its label, its job and what sets it, a reset for what's live on it |
| `strip-view.ts` | `stripHtml()`: a strip drawn from its model, its name, where it goes and what plays on it as the view says (`StripLook`), its slots, its sends, pan, fader, level bar, value, mute and solo; quiet where it's silent; `slotsHtml()`, its inserts' slots, each its device and what sets it; `muteSoloHtml()`, its mute and solo by its key |

Tested in node on HTML as text and on values, in its `test/` folder: `dom.test.js`, `desk-view.test.js`.
