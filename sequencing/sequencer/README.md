# libs/sequencing/sequencer

Patterns played on the grid, after Ableton's session view, for events of any kind. A **pattern** is a clip: `{ meter, bars, loop, events }`, its time signature (the master's when it's left out), its length in bars, whether it loops, and its events, each `{ start, duration, … }` in ticks, 96 to a quarter (`PPQ`), on the straight grid. A rest isn't stored: it's the gap between events. A pattern's events are any events (`PatternEvent`, `Pattern<Event>`): where each starts and how long it lasts, whatever else it carries passed on, never read. What's played is kept as a **record**, which is a pattern too. Pure: no Web Audio, on the clock (`libs/sequencing/clock/`) and `libs/base/util/` alone; the MIDI sequencer, notes and their messages, the input, the stream, the recorder and the recording player, is on top of it (`libs/sequencing/midi-sequencer/README.md`). Its tests in its `test/` folder. It's given the clock's grid as functions, so a pattern placed again is always the same. Why it's built so: `mockup/architecture/sequencer.md`.

```js
import { ClipLauncher, TrackPool, patternOf, upcoming } from './index.ts';
const next = upcoming(patternOf(record, cycle), at, 3);             // a record's next three events from a place, round the loop
const track = pool.place(owner, from, until);                          // a play on a pool's track, its index (a TrackPool)
const lights = new ClipLauncher({ grid, play: (track, cue, at) => light(track, cue.color, at) });
lights.launch({ track: 'lamp', pattern: cues });                         // a clip of cues looping on its track
lights.step(i, t);                                                       // each 16th: every clip's events due there handed on
```

| | |
|---|---|
| `lengthOf(pat, meter)` | a pattern's length in ticks, `libs/sequencing/clock/`'s (`PPQ`, `STEP`, `barTicks()`, `startsBar()`, the clock's) |
| `patternOf(record, cycle)` | a record as the pattern it is: from the one of its first bar, whole cycles long, looping where it was played (`from`) |
| `upcoming(pat, at, n)` | a looping pattern's next events from a place, round the loop |
| `unmute(pat, at, rand)` | a play's run of a pattern's events from a place, 1 up to all, round the loop; one draw |
| `patternsIn(bank)`, `cycleOf(patterns, meter)` | every pattern in a bank, anything with events, in it or in its sets; its cycle, its longest pattern in whole bars, so what's recorded is as long or longer in whole cycles |
| `whole(pat, at)` | a pattern played whole, as a launched clip: from its first event to its last, once, its first in the beat at `at`, each event in its place in the beat as kept, so its swing and offbeats stay; its place in the bar moves by whole beats |
| `due(pat, i)` | what a repeating pattern plays in the grid's 16th `i` |
| `liveEvents(pat, start, scale, from, to)` | a rhythm played live from where it started, at half, normal or double time (`scale`), its events between two places |
| `rescaled()` | where a rhythm would have started at another speed, so it runs on with no jump |
| `underWayAt(pat, start, scale, at)` | the event under way at a place, for a soloist coming in mid-note |
| `place(base, start, grid, from)`, `placeOn(…)`, `Grid` | place → clock time: from the beat it starts on (`'beat'`), or at its phrase's own place in the beat (`'phrase'`), so the swing falls on the same events. `grid`: `{ stepAt, timeAt }`, the clock's grid a pattern is placed on: where a clock time lies on it, in 16ths, and the clock time a place on it falls at. `placeOn()` the place on the grid in 16ths instead, for a phrase sent as its events come due, each timed then |
| `new ClipLauncher({ grid, play, started, ended })` (`launcher.ts`), `ClipGrid`, `LaunchedClip`, `ClipsTold`, `PlayEvent`, `Span`, `launch(clip)`, `stop(track)`, `step(i, t)` | `ClipGrid`: the grid a clip plays on, the clock's: its time signature, a 16th's swing, the clock time a place on it falls at, ticks in seconds. `LaunchedClip`: a clip as launched: the track it plays on, its pattern, and how it plays (`straight`, `once`). `PlayEvent`: an event handed on: its track, the event, the clock time it falls at, and its clip's launch. `ClipsTold`: a clip played once, told as it starts (`started`, with its `Span`, from its first event's time to its last's end) and as its last is handed on (`ended`). `Span`: `from` and `until`. Clips of events of any kind launched on tracks, one a track, each looping on the beat grid, or played once (`once`) from the next beat, its first event to its last, each in its place in the beat as kept (`whole()`); each 16th, every clip's events due there handed on (`play(track, event, at, launch)`) at the clock times they fall at, on the swing or straight under it, an event's own `straight` before its clip's, an event off its 16th where it falls; each launch numbered, a clip launched again in a track's place keeping it; a clip played once told as it starts, its span from its first event's time to its last's end (`started(track, launch, { from, until })`), and as its last is handed on (`ended(track, launch)`), not when it's launched over or stopped; a track any value its owner keys clips by |

**Track pools** (`track-pool.ts`): `new TrackPool()` gives a pool of tracks, overlapping plays allotted to them as a polysynth allots its voices, `{ tracks, place(owner, from, until, { join }), end(index, until) }`; `poolsFor(keys)` a pool for each key. `place()` puts a play on a track and returns its index: the track its owner played on last if that's free, else the lowest free one, else a new one. A track is free once the play it holds has died away (`until`), so it plays one at a time; `end()` ends a placement held open (`until` never) once its end is known, a held note let go; a play may `join` its owner's track while the owner still holds it, as notes from one place share a voice, the track held until the later end, how long a track is held a `Holding` (`holding.ts`), never while a play on it is held open, else the latest end; tracks are never taken away.

**Phrases** (`phrases.ts`): `new Phrases({ region, send, grid })`, a phrase of events kept by its places on the grid (`play(to, events)`, each a `DueEvent`, `{ start, event() }`: where it lies on the grid, in ticks, and the event as it comes due, worked out then), each event worked out and sent as its 16th comes (`step(i)`), those on a 16th handed out already at once; a hold or Stop hands out no 16th, so a phrase waits with the music; whether a clip's phrase still has events to send (`playing(clip)`), a clip's forgotten (`forget(clip)`), all (`clear()`).

Tested in node, in its `test/` folder (`test/sequencer.test.js`, `track-pool.test.js`, `phrases.test.js`, `launcher.test.js`, the launcher's on a light's cues, not notes, looping and played once); a phrase's speed and feel and its reshaping are music's, in a library of their own (`libs/music/phrasing/README.md`).
